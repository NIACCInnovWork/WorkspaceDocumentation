# Laser Cutting

## Overivew
The NIACC Innovation Workspace has two carbon dioxide laser cutters available for our members to use: the Full Spectrum PS48 and the Epilog Fusion Pro.
This documentation includes everything you need to know about the lasers such as which machine to choose, what materials can be used, and how to perform maintenance on the machines.

## Machine Summary
### Full Spectrum PS48
This was the first laser purchased by the NIACC Innovation Workspace and it is a powerful machine. 
Boasting a 48 inch by 36 in cutting bed and a 150 W laser tube, this laser has the power to cut through 1/4 inch material with ease and is still delicate enough to engrave a photo onto cardboard.
Visit the Full Spectrum PS48 Profile for more details.

### Epilog Fusion Pro
The Epilog Fusion Pro is fast, reliable, and precise making it an exceptional machine to get started on.
With a 24 inch by 24 in cutting bed and a 60 W laser tube, this machine is a bit smaller and less powerful than the Full Spectrum, but its speed and precision make it the more refined of the two.
Visit the Epilog Fusion Pro Profile for more details.
