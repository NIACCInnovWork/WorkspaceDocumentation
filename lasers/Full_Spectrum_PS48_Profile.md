# Full Spectrum PS48 Laser Cutter Profile

## Overview
The Full Spectrum Pro-Series 48" Laser (PS48) is the workspace's largest and most powerful carbon dioxide laser cutter. 
This is the ideal machine for large projects that require significant power such as cutting through quarter inch material.
However, the tradeoff is that this machine is not as quick or precise as the other workspace laser, the Epilog Fusion Edge.

## Laser Maintenance Resources
- [P-Series Bed Alignment Tutorial](https://youtu.be/B6KBXsEZBKM)
  - Occasionally the cutting bed get out of alignment due to a collision causing the belts to slip. Follow this tutorial to get it realigned.
- [Laser Talk Tidbits - Cleaning a Pro-Series Laser Mirror](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn7ouwk8Z6Y)
  - Periodically the workspace needs to cleans the lens to keep it clean and prevent it from cracking.
  - Soot can build up and can be burned onto the mirror or lens causing problems.
  - This video describes how to clean the lens and what can go wrong when maintanence isn't done properly.
- [Full Spectrum Laser ProLF 36x24 and 48x36 alignment video](https://youtu.be/bg9O3Pzpfxw)
  - This video shows the mirror alignment process for the PS48.
  - It uses a slightly older version of the machine, so the control interface and software are a bit different but the process and hardware alignment are the same.
