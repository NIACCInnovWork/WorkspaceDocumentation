# Laser Materials

## Approved Materials
| Material | Max Thickness | Notes | Warnings |
|---|---|---|---|
|Natural woods| 1/4" | Avoid oily or resinous woods | Be very careful about cutting oily or resinous woods as they may catch fire. Check with the workspace staff before cutting any finished woods.|
|Plywood or composite wood | 1/4" | These contain glue and may not cut as wel as solid wood. | | 
| MDF or engineered woods | 1/4" | These are okay to use but may experience a higher amount of charring when cut. | | 
| Paper or Cardstock | Thin | Cuts very well on the laser cutter and also very quickly. | You may need to turn off the air compressor when cutting paper. There is a significant fire risk if your power is set too high.|
